# Limitations

- Published comparisons are known-period development evidence, not independent confirmation, operational validation or state-of-the-art claims.
- PV snapshots may contain revisions. Event-time lag does not establish publication availability; capacity vintages and methodological continuity remain unresolved.
- GFS init-plus-six-hours approximates eligibility. Future observations, reanalysis and retrieval time are not historical availability evidence.
- National PV results do not establish site-level accuracy.
- TSFM pretraining overlap is uncertain; zero-shot does not imply contamination-free evaluation.
- Public aggregates and source code omit original frozen raw data, weights and prediction bundles. See [reproducibility](../reproducibility.md).
- APIs may evolve before 1.0. This site tracks main; published 0.1.0 metadata stays immutable.

No ensemble, uncertainty model, automatic retraining or production service is implied.
